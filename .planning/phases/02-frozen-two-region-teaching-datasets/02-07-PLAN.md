<objective>
Wire the sample-design points to the AlphaEarth embeddings and to their course label plus independent validation reference. This is where Pitfall 4 (scattered sampling) and Pitfall 6 (circular validation) get their concrete, testable prevention.

Purpose: `extract_aef.py` turns `(area, year)` + the fixed point set into `A00..A63` per point via exactly one windowed read. `attach_labels.py` turns the same point set into `label`/`label_source` (from the `worldcover_class` sample_design already carries) and an independently-sourced `imd_2018`/`validation_source`.
Output: `pipeline/extract_aef.py::extract_area_year`, `pipeline/attach_labels.py::attach_labels`, both unit-tested with synthetic inputs (no network) plus one network-marked slice test each.
</objective>
