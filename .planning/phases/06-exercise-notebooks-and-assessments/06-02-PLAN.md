setup block around it) should be blanked; omit markers to blank the whole cell. Do NOT reuse the

each file first to see current usage) unless the cell is also `task`-tagged, in which case just
drop the `solution` entry from that cell's tags list (don't delete the cell).

central — a similarity/cosine call, a prototype construction, a CV split and scoring call, a
classifier fit, a dequantization formula, a PCA fit — never setup/import/data-loading/plotting-
only/provenance-write/Path-A-skip-execution cells. Keep `checkpoint`, `wrong-conclusion`,
`parameters` tagged cells untouched (never tag these `task` — the checkpoint assert and the trap
must always execute).
